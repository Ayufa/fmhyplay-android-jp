Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

The short version

FMHYPlay has no account, no analytics, no ads of its own and no telemetry. It has no server: nothing you do in the app is sent to the FMHYPlay developers.

What stays on your device

Your favorites, My list, Continue watching (titles, sites and where you stopped), playlists and Xtream or Stalker logins you add, settings, and the WARP log. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Who the app talks to

  • The sites and playlists you open, like any web browser. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Catalog and subtitles: Cinemeta and Kitsu (titles and posters), OpenSubtitles (when you search subtitles).
  • Cloudflare WARP, when WARP is on: the app's traffic goes through Cloudflare.
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates.
  • Casting: your phone talks directly to the TV on your own Wi-Fi.

Simkl and PunchPlay (optional)

Only if you connect them in Settings: when you play a movie or episode opened from the catalog, FMHYPlay sends that service its IDs (IMDb), the season and episode, how far you are, and whether playback started, paused or stopped, so it can mark it watched in your history there. The sign-in token is stored only on your device. Disconnecting in Settings stops this; you can also revoke FMHYPlay in the service's own settings. Their privacy policies apply to what they keep.
